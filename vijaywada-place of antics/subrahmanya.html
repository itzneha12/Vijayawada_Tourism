01 — ABOUT THE TEMPLE

A place of faith,
prayer and devotion.

Subrahmanya Swamy is worshipped as the divine son of Lord Shiva and Goddess Parvati. Devotees visit temples dedicated to him to offer prayers and seek blessings.

If you're planning to visit this temple in Vijayawada, enjoy a peaceful spiritual experience and learn about the traditions associated with Lord Subrahmanya.
