Vijayawada's food culture is strongly connected with Andhra cuisine and South Indian favourites.

If you're visiting the city for the first time, these are the kinds of foods worth looking for.

02 — WHAT TO TRY
BREAKFAST

Idli, Dosa & Vada

Classic South Indian breakfast options that are easy to find around Vijayawada.

₹50 — ₹150
ANDHRA CUISINE

Andhra Meals

Rice, curries, dal, vegetables, pickles and other traditional Andhra flavours.

₹100 — ₹300
LOCAL FAVOURITE

Pesarattu

A popular Andhra breakfast made with green gram and commonly served with chutney.

₹60 — ₹150
MEAL

Biryani

A popular choice for lunch and dinner, with many restaurants offering their own style.

₹180 — ₹400
