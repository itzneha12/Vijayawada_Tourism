01 — ABOUT

History carved
into stone.

The Undavalli Caves are a historic rock-cut cave complex located near Vijayawada.

The caves are known for their impressive architecture, carved pillars, sculptures and multi-level interiors.

The site offers visitors an opportunity to see how art, architecture and religious traditions were expressed through rock-cut construction.
