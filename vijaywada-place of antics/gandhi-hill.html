01 — ABOUT

A hill above
the city.

Gandhi Hill is a prominent hilltop attraction in Vijayawada associated with Mahatma Gandhi and the city's history.

The elevated location provides visitors with an opportunity to see Vijayawada from a different perspective.

It is a good addition to a sightseeing trip when you want a combination of history, views and a quieter atmosphere.
