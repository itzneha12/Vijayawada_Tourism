01 — ABOUT

Where history
meets the hills.

Kondapalli Fort is a historic fortification located on the Kondapalli hills near Vijayawada.

The fort has played an important role in the region's history and is known for its strategic hilltop location.

Today, visitors can explore its historic structures while enjoying views of the surrounding landscape.
