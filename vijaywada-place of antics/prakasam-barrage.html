01 — ABOUT

A landmark
over the Krishna.

Prakasam Barrage is one of Vijayawada's best-known landmarks, stretching across the Krishna River.

The barrage connects the city with the surrounding river landscape and plays an important role in the region's irrigation system.

For visitors, the area is particularly attractive around the evening, when the river and surrounding city create a scenic backdrop.
