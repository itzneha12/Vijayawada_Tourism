01 — ABOUT

Escape the
city for a while.

Bhavani Island is a riverside recreation destination located in the Krishna River near Vijayawada.

Surrounded by water and greenery, the island offers visitors a different side of Vijayawada away from the busy city streets.

It can be a good choice for visitors looking for a relaxed outing, family activities or time outdoors.
