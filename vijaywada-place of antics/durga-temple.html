01 — ABOUT

The spiritual
heart of Vijayawada.

Sri Durga Malleswara Swamy Varla Devasthanam, commonly known as Kanaka Durga Temple, is situated on Indrakeeladri hill in Vijayawada.

The temple is associated with Goddess Kanaka Durga and is an important pilgrimage destination. It overlooks the Krishna River and is one of Vijayawada's best-known landmarks.

The temple is especially significant during Navaratri and Dasara celebrations, when large numbers of devotees visit.
