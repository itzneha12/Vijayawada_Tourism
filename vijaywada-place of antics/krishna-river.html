01 — ABOUT

The river
defines the city.

The Krishna River is one of the defining natural features of Vijayawada and plays an important role in the city's landscape.

Several of Vijayawada's well-known attractions are connected to the river, making it an important part of the city's tourism experience.

From the Prakasam Barrage to riverside viewpoints and nearby islands, the Krishna offers plenty of ways to experience Vijayawada outdoors.

04 — VISITOR TIPS

responsibly.

💧

Stay Hydrated

🌞

Plan Around the Heat

Early mornings and evenings can be more comfortable for outdoor activities.

🚤

Don't leave plastic or waste behind. Keep the riverside beautiful.
